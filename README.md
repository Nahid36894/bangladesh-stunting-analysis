# Child Stunting Analysis — Bangladesh DHS 2017-18

## Research Question
What are the determinants of child stunting 
in Bangladesh?

## Data
Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey (BDHS) 2017-18
n = 7,849 children under 5 years

## Methods
- Multilevel Logistic Regression (glmer)
- Level 1: Children
- Level 2: Cluster/Community
- ICC to measure cluster-level variation
- R packages: haven, lme4, performance

## Key Findings
- Stunting prevalence: 31.4%
- Mother's education → stunting odds 19.1% lower per level
- Household wealth → stunting odds 21.1% lower per level
- Cluster-level ICC = 3.4% (family factors > area factors)
- Both results statistically significant (p < 0.001)

## Policy Implication
Improving maternal education and household 
wealth are key strategies to reduce child 
stunting in Bangladesh.
